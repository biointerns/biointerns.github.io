**Aalto University Science Institute International Summer Research Programme**

_Open to students around the world_

**Where**: Aalto University, Otaniemi, Espoo / Helsinki, Finland

**When**: 12 weeks between June and August 2027

**Application Deadline**: 31/01/2027

**Benefits**: 2000+ EUR/month (depending on experience) to cover accommodation and personal expenses + 400/800 EUR to cover travel costs

**Website**: [Aalto International Summer Research Programme](https://www.aalto.fi/en/aalto-university-science-institute-asci/aalto-university-science-institute-international-summer-research-programme)
